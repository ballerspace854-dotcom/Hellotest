# Pawzi — 20 solution ads

Twenty new 1080 × 1080 Facebook feed ads for owners of high-energy dogs with IVDD on crate rest.

Each ad opens with the owner's worry, helplessness or sadness about their dog's back pain. Pawzi appears second as a way to help ease back pain at home during prescribed rest.

- `statics/`: 20 finished PNG ads.
- `contact-sheet.pdf`: five pages with four ads per page.
- `copy-index.csv`: every ad's problem, emotional opening and solution copy.
- `index.html`: local image gallery.
- `editable/` and `assets/`: SVG compositions with the original Pawzi logo placed separately. The generated visual includes its typography.

The ZIP includes the finished PNGs, contact sheet, gallery, copy index and source notes. SVGs and visual assets are available separately in the repository.
