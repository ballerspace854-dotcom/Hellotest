# Pawzi — 20 Facebook feed ads

Twenty new square PNG ads, 1080 × 1080, for owners of high-energy dogs with IVDD on crate rest who are considering red light therapy.

- `statics/`: the 20 finished ads.
- `contact-sheet.pdf`: five pages with four ads per page.
- `copy-index.csv`: artwork copy and product-page feature references.
- `index.html`: a gallery to open locally.
- `editable/` and `assets/`: the original visual layers with the exact Pawzi logo placed separately; the generated artwork includes its typography.

The visuals include product comparisons, changes to crate-rest setups and routines, and new everyday home scenes. Features come from the current Pawzi product page: cordless USB-C use, auto shut-off, no need to hold or strap the pad, 60 LEDs, 660 nm red and 850 nm near-infrared, $99 single-pad price and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Check price and offer terms before running the ads.

The ZIP contains the finished PNGs, contact sheet, gallery, copy index and source notes. The SVG compositions and visual assets are available separately in the repository.
