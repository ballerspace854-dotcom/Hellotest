# Afterwell — 30 parent-focused ads

Thirty new 1080 × 1080 Facebook feed ads for parents of autistic children with eczema. Each opens with a familiar discomfort or care-routine problem, then states Afterwell's moisturizing or soothing purpose in plain words.

- `statics/`: the 30 finished PNG ads.
- `contact-sheet.pdf`: eight pages with up to four ads per page.
- `copy-index.csv`: visible copy, research references and evidence notes.
- `index.html`: local gallery after unzipping.
- `editable/` and `assets/`: SVG compositions and visual layers in the repository. Typography within generated scenes is part of the artwork.

Branding is provisional: no official Afterwell logo or final label was supplied. The ads use a simple Afterwell wordmark and an illustrative Afterwell label on the supplied cobalt-jar/white-cap product appearance. The competitor's name, healing slogan and wave mark are not used.

The ZIP includes the finished PNGs, contact sheet, gallery, copy index and source notes. SVGs and visual assets are available separately in the repository.
