# Pawzi — 30 static ads

Made for owners of high-energy dogs with IVDD who are on crate rest. The ads meet people who already know about rest and vet care and are considering an at-home red light pad.

## Open the batch

- `statics/`: 30 individual square PNG ads, numbered 01–30.
- `index.html`: open in a browser to browse the images and download individual files.
- `pawzi-contact-sheet.pdf`: five pages showing all 30 concepts, six per page.
- `ad-copy.csv`: copy and creative index, readable in a spreadsheet.
- `ad-index.json`: the same index for editing or automation.
- `source-notes.md`: research quote attribution and product evidence.

The final PNGs are the artwork. The copy index is a convenient text reference. Before publishing, review the final artwork and use the corresponding product page as the ad destination:

https://trypawzi.com/products/pawzi%E2%84%A2-red-light-pad

## What varies

The batch includes home photographs, overhead apartment views, desk-side and evening scenes, photo sequences, a checklist, a floor plan, editorial quote cards, hardware details, routine/setup before-and-afters, and comparisons. The whole batch follows the supplied product image: rounded black textile pad, gray paw-and-arcs mark, black strap, square silver button, and red light underneath.

## Quotes and images

Quote-led ads use credited owner words from the supplied Reddit research, not Pawzi customer reviews. No verified landing-page testimonials were supplied. The product views and dog scenes are AI-created illustrations based on the user-supplied product image, rather than customer photographs.

Before-and-afters show the addition of a light session or pad to an unchanged rest setup. They do not show a health result. Comparisons describe handling, place of use, or the difference between clinic lasers and a home LED pad; they do not claim equal treatment or results. The ads do not reproduce the unverified medical seal, graph, percentages, or medication-reduction claims printed on the supplied product image.

The product page was blocked by this workspace's network proxy during production. The user-supplied image and two supplied PDFs were used for this batch. Website access is no longer needed to complete these images.
