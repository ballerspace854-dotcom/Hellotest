# Pawzi — 20 new Facebook feed ads

20 new 1080 × 1080 ads for owners of high-energy dogs with IVDD on crate rest who are considering red light therapy.

New home scenes, product comparisons and before-and-after setups show the features from the Pawzi page: cordless USB-C use, auto shut-off, a pad that lays flat, and 60 LEDs with 660 nm and 850 nm light. Original Pawzi logo and red brand accents throughout.
